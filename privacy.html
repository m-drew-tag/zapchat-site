The short version

Zapchat links to your existing WhatsApp account as a companion device. To do that, your messages pass through a relay server operated by TAG in the United States. Message content is decrypted on that server — this is unavoidable for any companion client that is not WhatsApp's own. Content is queued on that server only until your device has it: at most 12 hours once delivered, and at most 7 days if your device has not come to collect it. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or training anything, and it is never sold.

What TAG's server handles

  • Message content — text, photos, videos, voice notes, documents, locations, contact cards and polls, in both directions, with the sender's name and number. Held in a queue written to disk, so that a restart does not cost you messages your phone had not collected yet.
  • Messages you send — also kept for 7 days, so that WhatsApp can ask for one again if a recipient's phone failed to decrypt it. This is a WhatsApp mechanism; without it such a message is lost without any sign.
  • Calls — voice and video calls are carried through the relay. Calls are not recorded. For 7 days the relay keeps technical diagnostics of each call: its identifiers, the WhatsApp ids of the people in it, the call's encryption key and packet statistics. No audio and no video.
  • Your WhatsApp session — the credentials that let Zapchat act as your companion device, and what WhatsApp sends a linked device about your account: your contacts' names and numbers, your groups and their members, and your chat settings.
  • Numbers you look up — when you type or pick a phone number to start a chat, that number is sent to the relay to find its WhatsApp account. Your address book is read on your phone only, to show names, and is never uploaded.
  • Your device's push token, so the relay can notify your phone.
  • Server logs — the relay's log records what it does, and during the current testing period it is not filtered: it can contain chat names, contact names, phone numbers and WhatsApp ids, and parts of messages. It is kept for 21 days and read only to diagnose problems.
  • Problem reports — when you use Settings → Report a Problem, the report carries what you write, your app and iOS version, your device model and a recent diagnostic log from the app. That log may include chat names and parts of messages. Kept for 30 days.
  • Emails you send to support@tag.org.

Message content can reveal sensitive things — health, religion, beliefs, sexual life. Zapchat does not look for them, sort by them or use them for anything; they pass through like any other content, under the limits above.

Notifications

When a message arrives, the notification carries the sender's name, the chat or group name, and the message text so that you can read it without opening the app. A notification for an incoming call carries the caller's phone number. All of that passes through Apple's push service on the way to your device.

A muted or archived chat is the exception: its notification carries no text at all, because it is not meant to interrupt you.

Who else handles it

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — your messages and calls travel over WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. WhatsApp decides for itself what it does with your account; TAG does not share anything with it beyond what a linked device must.
  • Dedicated.com — hosts the relay server, in New York.
  • Cloudflare — carries the connection between the app and the relay, and serves this website. The encrypted connection ends at Cloudflare's network before it reaches the relay, so Cloudflare's systems handle that traffic, message content included, in transit.
  • Apple — delivers notifications (see above).
  • Microsoft — hosts the support@tag.org mailbox.

TAG discloses data to authorities only where the law compels it.

Why, and on what basis

  • To provide the service you asked for — relaying your messages and calls, notifications, contact lookups: because it is necessary to provide Zapchat to you.
  • Other people's messages to you, the server logs, call diagnostics, problem reports and backups: TAG's legitimate interest in delivering what was sent to you, keeping the service working and secure, and fixing what breaks — limited to the retention below.
  • Problem reports: you choose to send one.

Zapchat makes no automated decisions about you and builds no profile of you.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, to restrict or object to its processing, and to take it elsewhere. Write to support@tag.org from any address and tell us the phone number of your linked account; to confirm it is you, we will ask you to send a code from that WhatsApp account. We answer within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, tell us first and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Other people

Messages you receive were written by people who did not choose Zapchat. Their content, names and numbers pass through the same relay under the same terms and limits described here, and they have the same rights: anyone can write to support@tag.org.

Security

The app reaches the relay only over encrypted connections, the relay has no port open to the internet, and its data is accessible only to TAG's operators. Message content is decrypted on the relay, as described above.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no sale or sharing of your data with advertisers.
  • No analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no crash reporters that carry content.
  • No use of your messages to train machine-learning models.

Children

Zapchat is not directed at children and requires an existing WhatsApp account, which WhatsApp itself age-restricts. If we learn that a child under 13 is using it, we delete that session.

Not affiliated with WhatsApp

Zapchat is an independent client. It is not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with WhatsApp LLC or Meta. Your use of WhatsApp remains governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
